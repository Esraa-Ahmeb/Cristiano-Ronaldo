juventus

Cristiano Ronaldo joined Juventus in 2018, bringing his experience and winning mentality to the Italian giants. During his three seasons in Turin, he scored 101 goals in 134 appearances, becoming the club’s top scorer each season Ronaldo helped Juventus win two Serie A titles, two Supercoppa Italiana trophies, and a Coppa Italia. Despite not winning the Champions League, he delivered many memorable performances and maintained his elite level. He was named Serie A Player of the Year twice and continued to break records, showing his consistency and professionalism. Ronaldo left Juventus in 2021, leaving a strong legacy in Italian football.
