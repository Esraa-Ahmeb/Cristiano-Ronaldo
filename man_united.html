Manchester United

Cristiano Ronaldo began his first spell at Manchester United in 2003,joining from Sporting Lisbon at just 18 years old. Under Sir Alex Ferguson’s guidance, he quickly developed into one of the world’s top talents. Known for his incredible speed, skill, and work ethic, Ronaldo played a key role in United’s success. He helped the team win three Premier League titles, an FA Cup, two League Cups, and the UEFA Champions League in 2008. During his six seasons at the club, he scored 118 goals in 292 appearances. His performances earned him the 2008 Ballon d’Or before he joined Real Madrid. Cristiano Ronaldo returned to Manchester United in 2021 after 12 years away, rejoining the club with great excitement from fans. In his first season back, he scored 24 goals in all competitions, including several crucial ones in the Premier League and Champions League. Despite his strong individual performances, the team struggled to find consistent success. The 2022–2023 season brought challenges, including limited playing time and tension with management. In November 2022, his contract was mutually terminated. Though his second spell was shorter and less successful, Ronaldo still showed flashes of brilliance and remained a global icon throughout his time there.
