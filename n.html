His arrival brought global attention to the league and boosted its popularity. Ronaldo quickly made an impact, scoring goals consistently and showing his leadership on and off the pitch. He helped Al Nassr compete strongly in domestic and regional competitions, aiming to bring major titles to the club. Beyond his performances, Ronaldo's presence inspired local players and fans, playing a key role in the growth of football in Saudi Arabia.
