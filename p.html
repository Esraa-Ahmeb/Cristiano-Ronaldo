Cristiano Ronaldo is one of Portugal’s greatest football players. He started playing for the national team in 2003. Since then, he has played in many big tournaments, including the FIFA World Cup and the UEFA European Championship. In 2016, he helped Portugal win their first major trophy: Euro 2016. Although he was injured in the final, he cheered and supported his team from the sidelines. Portugal beat France 1-0 and made history. In 2019, Ronaldo and Portugal won the UEFA Nations League, another important title. Ronaldo also holds many records. He is the top scorer for Portugal, with more than 130 international goals. He is also the player with the most matches for the national team. Ronaldo’s leadership, passion, and hard work have made him a hero in Portugal. His story is one of dedication and success, and he continues to inspire young players around the world.
