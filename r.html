real Madrid

Cristiano Ronaldo joined Real Madrid in 2009 for a then-world record fee. Over nine seasons, he became the club’s all-time top scorer with 450 goals in 438 appearances. Known for his goal-scoring ability, athleticism, and leadership, Ronaldo played a crucial role in one of the most successful eras in the club’s history. He won four Champions League titles, two La Liga titles, two Copa del Rey trophies, and three FIFA Club World Cups. Ronaldo also won four Ballon d'Or awards during his time in Madrid. His incredible impact made him a legend at the Santiago Bernabéu before leaving in 2018.
